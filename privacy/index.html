Shikaku Daily: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Shikaku Daily is a puzzle game for Android made by HydroZeppelin, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, so it is short.

In brief: the game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only information it sends to me is what is needed to put your puzzle times on the daily leaderboards.

What the game sends to its server

When you finish one of the day's puzzles, the game sends the following to the game's own server so that your time can appear on that day's leaderboard:

WhatDetails
A player IDIf you have not signed in, this is a random ID created on your device. It is not linked to your name, email address or any other account. If you have signed in to Google Play Games, it is your Play Games player ID.
A display nameIf you have not signed in, a name made up by the game (such as "BraveSquare56"). If you have signed in, your Play Games gamer name. A supporter's chosen pet is sent with the name so that it can be shown beside it.
Your puzzle timesHow long each puzzle took, which puzzle it was, and the date.

Your display name and times are shown to other players on the leaderboards. Nothing else about you is shown, and the server does not receive your email address, real name, location, contacts or device identifiers.

When the game shows you a leaderboard, it sends your player ID so the server can find your place on the board. Fetching the day's puzzles sends nothing about you.

The server is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web service, Cloudflare processes the IP address your device connects from in order to deliver the response. I do not store IP addresses or use them to identify anyone.

Google services the game uses

The game uses the following Google services. Google handles the information involved under Google's own privacy policy.

ServiceWhat it is used for
Google Play Games (optional)If you choose to sign in, the game uses your Play Games player ID and gamer name as described above, and keeps a copy of your records (streak, finished days, best times and settings) in your own Google account so they follow you to a new phone. If you do not sign in, none of this happens.
Google Play BillingThe optional Supporter Pack is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment. I never see your payment details; the game is only told whether your Google account owns the pack.
Android backupIf backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may keep a copy of the game's saved data (records, settings and progress) in your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game.

What stays on your device

Your progress, streak, records, settings and downloaded puzzles are stored on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What the game does not do

  • It shows no advertising and does not use your advertising ID.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

The game checks whether your device has an internet connection, only so that it can tell you when it cannot load the day's puzzles because you are offline.

Reporting a problem

The "report a problem" option in the game's settings opens your own email app with a message addressed to me. The message is pre-filled with the game's version, your Android version, the puzzle date and your player ID, to help me find the problem. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email, and you can edit it first.

How long information is kept

Leaderboard entries (player ID, display name and times) are kept on the server so that past leaderboards remain available. Information in your Google account is kept there until you delete it or Google removes it under its own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play without signing in. Your times then appear under a made-up name.
  • You can ask me to delete your leaderboard entries, or to send you a copy of them. Email me at the address below with your player ID (it is included in the "report a problem" email) or your Play Games gamer name.
  • You can delete the copy of your records kept with Play Games from the Play Games app, under its settings for your account and game data.
  • If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with similar laws, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and collects no more information from any player than is described above. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and the date at the top.
